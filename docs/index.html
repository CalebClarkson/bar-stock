Stocktake on your phone

This page needs your venue's product list once. It comes from the Bar Stock app on the work PC.

  1. On the work PC, open Stocktake and press Copy phone link.
  2. Email or message that link to yourself, and open it on this phone.
  3. On an iPhone, the home screen icon keeps its own copy, so open the icon and paste the link below.

Tip: add this page to your home screen (Share > Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or the browser menu > Add to Home screen on Android). It then works like an app, even with no signal.
