BAR STOCK - END OF SHIFT

1. Square Dashboard > Reports > Item sales. Choose today's date.
   Press Export > Item sales detail CSV.
2. Open Bar Stock (shortcut on the desktop) and sign in.
3. Press Upload Square sales and choose the file (or drag it onto the box).
   Uploading the same file twice is safe.
4. Spills or anything not rung through Square: press Log wastage or staff drink.
5. Made a batch? Press Make a batch.

Managers, if the front page shows a yellow note:
- "Square items not linked": press Link them, then pick the drink or product,
  or press No stock for food and anything else that isn't bar stock.
- "Comps with no reason": press Sort them and pick a reason for each.

New drink or special: Settings > Drinks & specials > + New special.
Give it the same name as its Square button and it links itself.
Deliveries: Settings > Deliveries, once checked in.
Stocktake: press Stocktake, count Bar and Cellar, then press Finish stocktake once.
Do not add a delivery during a stocktake.
