BAR STOCK - END OF SHIFT

1. Square Dashboard > Reports > Item sales. Choose today's date.
   Press Export > Item sales detail CSV.
2. Open Bar Stock (shortcut on the desktop) and sign in.
3. Square sales tab > choose the file > Import.
   Importing the same day twice is safe.
4. If the Mapping tab shows a number: link each new drink to its stock,
   or press No stock for food and anything else that isn't bar stock.
5. Reports tab: give any comps a reason (wastage, overpour, training or staff drinks).
6. Wastage tab: log spills and anything not rung through Square.
7. Batches tab: log any batch made.

Deliveries: use the Deliveries tab once checked in.
Stocktake: on the Count tab, count Bar and Cellar, then press Finish stocktake once.
Do not add a delivery during a stocktake.
